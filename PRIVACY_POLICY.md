# Privacy Policy for RentLedger

**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026

RentLedger ("the Application") is built as a private, offline personal and commercial rental accounting tool designed specifically for property owners and landlords.

---

## 1. Principles of Privacy

- **Local-First & Offline Storage**: All properties, tenants, rental records, financial transactions, security deposits, and accounting logs are stored exclusively in an on-device SQLite database on your device.
- **Zero Cloud Transmission**: The Application does not maintain any backend servers, cloud databases, or third-party synchronization APIs. Your financial data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the iOS Share sheet to export a backup or CSV file.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Advertisements**: The Application contains zero tracking software, zero advertising SDKs, and zero behavioral telemetry.
- **No Account or Registration Required**: You do not need to create an account, log in with an email address, or provide any personal identity details to use the application.

---

## 2. Information Handled by the Application

The Application processes the following categories of data solely on your device:
1. **Property & Unit Information**: Property names, addresses, construction/active status, and landlord notes.
2. **Tenant Records**: Names, contact phone numbers/emails, lease periods, monthly rent figures, liability adjustments, and opening balances.
3. **Financial Records**: Income transactions, operational expenses, property development/construction capital expenditures (materials, civil labor, contractor payouts), rent payment allocations, and deposit histories.
4. **Device Features**: If enabled, Apple's LocalAuthentication framework (Face ID, Touch ID, or Passcode) is used strictly on-device to lock and unlock the Application. The Application never reads, collects, or stores biometric images or fingerprints.

---

## 3. Data Export & Backups

You retain complete ownership and custody of your records at all times. The Application includes tools to export your data in human-readable CSV and JSON formats. When you choose to export or backup your data, the file is transferred through standard iOS system share controls (e.g., Apple AirDrop, Files app, Mail) under your direct supervision.

---

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since no data is stored on remote servers, uninstalling the Application or choosing the "Reset All Ledger Data" option in the Settings tab permanently deletes all records from the device.

---

## 5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy or the Application's local data storage architecture, please contact the developer via the official repository at [https://github.com/aadiupa/rent-ledger](https://github.com/aadiupa/rent-ledger).
