## 4. App Description

RentLedger is an iPhone-first rental accounting app crafted for landlords, property owners, and shop complex managers in India. Combining the simplicity of a digital khata with a dual-entry cashbook, RentLedger gives you complete clarity over your properties without subscription fees or privacy compromises.

### 🌟 KEY HIGHLIGHTS:

- **100% Private & Offline:** Your financial data stays on your iPhone inside a local SQLite database. No logins, no server sync, no cloud subscriptions, and zero tracking.
- **Accurate Arrears Tracking:** Distinguishes rent due from past arrears. Never counts future rent as overdue, and guarantees security deposits do not erroneously offset monthly income.
- **Construction & Expense Capex:** Track operational maintenance, repair bills, and capital expenditures for new property construction (cement, steel, civil labor, contractor payouts).
- **Flexible Rent Liability Control:** Adjust rent liabilities at both tenant level (contract rent, due day, opening arrears) and charge level (custom monthly dues, waivers, due dates).
- **Multi-Property & Unit Portfolio:** Manage active rental properties, units, and ongoing construction developments with safe property deletion.
- **Lifetime Available Cash:** Real cashbook calculation carrying forward available bank/cash balance across months.
- **Deposit Liability Register:** Clear record of security deposits received, refunded, or adjusted for damages.
- **Smart Payment Allocations:** Allocate rent receipts to oldest unpaid charges automatically or customize per charge.
- **Instant CSV & JSON Export:** Generate clean CSV spreadsheets for tax filings and Excel/Google Sheets, plus one-tap JSON backups.
- **Face ID Security:** Protect your records with biometric app lock.

### 🏢 DESIGNED FOR:
- Independent residential landlords
- Commercial shop & office owners
- Property developers building new rental units
- PG & hostel managers
- Real estate investors seeking offline accounting
